A legal defense is an excuse engineered for an audience that starts
skeptical and gets to ask follow-up questions. That constraint changes
everything about how it's built.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lecture, you will be able to:

- identify what a legal defense keeps and discards from an ordinary excuse
- explain why diffusion of responsibility dominates legal strategy over denial
- anticipate the follow-up questions a hostile, skeptical audience will ask
