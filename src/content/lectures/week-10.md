The taxonomy from week 1 was built mostly from Western corporate and
courtroom material. This week tests it against face-saving norms it wasn't
designed around.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lecture, you will be able to:

- distinguish face-saving from an excuse, and explain how the two concepts relate
- test denial, justification, and diffusion of responsibility against a non-Western case
- identify which of the taxonomy's original assumptions the cross-cultural strain exposes

## Outline

- face-saving as a distinct, related concept to the excuse
- where denial/justification/diffusion travels, and where it breaks down
- what the strain in the taxonomy tells us about its original assumptions
