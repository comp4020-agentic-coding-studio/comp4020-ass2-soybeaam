## The brief

> Find a company's non-apology, and write the apology it should have been.

Corporate statements are the purest form of the non-apology, because they are
drafted by people who know exactly what they're avoiding. Find one, quote it
in full, and rewrite it as a statement that would actually count as an
apology under week 5's definition — then explain every change you made and
why.

## What you submit

The original (quoted, cited), your rewrite, and a commentary of 300–400 words
naming each device you removed (passive voice, hedges, agentless
constructions) and what you replaced it with.
