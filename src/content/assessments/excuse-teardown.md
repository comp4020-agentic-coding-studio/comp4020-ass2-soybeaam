## The brief

> Take one real excuse apart and show your working.

Pick a real excuse — yours, a public figure's, a company's — and analyse it
using the vocabulary built in weeks 1–3: the taxonomy of denial,
justification, and diffusion, and the grammatical devices (passive voice,
hedges, nominalisation) from week 2. The strongest submissions show where the
excuse resists the taxonomy, not just where it fits it.

## What you submit

A 600–800 word written analysis, quoting the excuse in full at the top, with
its source cited. Reference the taxonomy and the grammar devices by name.
