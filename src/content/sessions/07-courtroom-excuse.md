## Before the workshop

Read the short case brief linked from this week's [lecture](/lectures/week-07/).

## In the workshop

A mock cross-examination, run in pairs and then as a whole class: one person
gives a legal-style defense, the room cross-examines it the way opposing
counsel would. The goal is to feel the difference between an excuse that
persuades a friend and one built to survive a hostile audience.

## Afterwards

Start thinking about your case-study group for week 9 — groups of three to
four, self-selected, due to be finalised by week 8.
