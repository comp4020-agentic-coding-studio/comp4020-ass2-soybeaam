# Machine Confabulation and the Graceful Exit

Week 11 — an excuse with no excuse-maker behind it

---

## A useful control case

Ask a language model to justify a wrong answer, and it produces
something shaped exactly like an excuse — with no self-interest, no
dissonance to resolve, no reputation to protect.

---

## What confabulation isolates

If an excuse-shaped account can appear without a motivated speaker,
then the *mechanics* of an excuse — structure, plausibility, fitting
the taxonomy's categories — are separable from the psychology that
usually produces one.

---

## Structure without a stake

The model isn't lying, in the week-3 sense — there's no belief it's
protecting. It's also not dissonant — there's nothing to resolve.
It's the shape of the taxonomy, running with nobody home.
