# The Corporate Non-Apology

Week 5: a three-part test for a genuine apology

---

## What counts as a real apology?

For this course: a statement that names a fact, accepts an agent, and
states a consequence.

Almost no corporate statement clears all three. That gap is where the
analysis lives.

---

## The three-part test

1. Names a fact: what actually happened, specifically
2. Accepts an agent: who did it, without hedging
3. States a consequence: what happens next because of it

---

## "Mistakes were made"

Fails on **agent**. A fact is gestured at; nobody owns it.

## "We regret the impact this had"

Fails on **fact**. It names a feeling about an unspecified event, not
the event itself.
