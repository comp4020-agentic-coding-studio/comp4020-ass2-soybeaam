# Blame-Shifting and Scapegoats

Week 8 — when diffusion of responsibility happens to a group, not a person

---

## Diffusion scales

An individual manufactures a passive-voice sentence. A group under
pressure can manufacture a person instead.

---

## What a scapegoat needs

The pattern only works within a narrow band:

- plausible enough that the accusation isn't laughed off
- **not** sympathetic enough that the group turns on the accuser instead

Miss either condition and the scapegoating fails.

---

## It's still the same taxonomy

A scapegoat is diffusion of responsibility with a name attached to
the "someone else" — the group hasn't invented a new move, it's
performing week 1's third category collectively.
