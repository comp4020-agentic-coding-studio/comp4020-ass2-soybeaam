# Closing lecture

Week 12 — one semester of denial, justification, diffusion

---

## No new material

This is a retrospective, not a new topic. The workshop that follows
is the live capstone defenses.

---

## What held up

Denial, justification, and diffusion of responsibility recurred in
every register the course tested them against — playground, legal
brief, corporate statement, historical record.

---

## What strained

Week 10's cross-cultural material and week 11's machine confabulation
both pushed on the taxonomy's individualist, motivated-speaker
assumptions — and both showed exactly where those assumptions live.
