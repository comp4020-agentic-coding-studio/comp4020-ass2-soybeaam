# The Courtroom Excuse

Week 7: excuses engineered for a skeptical, questioning audience

---

## A different constraint

Most excuses in this course are told to an audience that might be
sympathetic. A legal defense is built for one that starts skeptical
and gets to interrogate it.

That constraint changes what survives.

---

## What a legal defense keeps

The same underlying moves, denial, justification and diffusion of
responsibility, are still there. Nothing new is invented.

---

## What it discards

Hedges and vagueness. Anything a hostile cross-examiner can pull
apart gets stripped out before it's ever said in the room.

---

## Why diffusion dominates

Denial is falsifiable by evidence. Diffusion of responsibility,
"others share the blame", survives scrutiny better, because it
doesn't require the core fact to be false.
