# Small Excuses, Big Tells

Week 4 — what children's excuses reveal about the taxonomy

---

## A fixed order

Children don't pick up denial, justification, and diffusion of
responsibility all at once. They arrive in a fairly consistent order.

That order is a map of which parts are instinctive and which are learned.

---

## First: denial

"I didn't do it" arrives before a child can construct anything more
sophisticated. No rule-modelling required — just the claim itself.

---

## Then: justification

Justification needs more: the child has to model the rule they're
being judged against before they can argue the rule shouldn't apply
here.

That modelling is a real cognitive step, and it shows up late.

---

## Last: diffusion of responsibility

"Everyone was doing it" or "he started it" is the most sophisticated
of the three — it requires reasoning about other people's culpability,
not just your own.

It's also the last one to appear.
