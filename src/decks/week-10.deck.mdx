# Excuse-Making Across Cultures

Week 10: stress-testing a taxonomy built on Western material

---

## Where the taxonomy came from

Denial, justification, diffusion of responsibility were built mostly
from Western corporate and courtroom examples. Today we test it
against something it wasn't designed around.

---

## Face-saving isn't an excuse

It's a related but distinct concept: preserving standing for
everyone in an exchange, not defending a specific claim of
innocence.

The two get confused because they can look similar from outside.

---

## Where the taxonomy travels, and where it doesn't

Denial and justification show up recognisably across the case.
Diffusion of responsibility behaves differently once the unit of
accountability is the group rather than the individual by default.

# The taxonomy strains exactly where it assumed an individual, blame-owning speaker.

## What the strain tells us

A framework's blind spots are diagnostic. Where week 1's categories
stop fitting cleanly is where its Western, individualist starting
assumptions are showing.
