# Policies and support

## Late work

Work handed in late loses 5% of the available marks per day, for up to five
days, after which it is not accepted. This is a real deadline, not a rhetorical
one — if you want more time, ask for it before the deadline, in writing, with
a reason. We will not grade the reason on its rhetorical merits.

## Extensions

Extensions are granted for documented illness, bereavement, or equivalent
serious disruption. Email the convenor before the deadline where possible.
"My excuse-writing course made me realise my excuse wasn't good enough" is,
regrettably, not grounds for an extension — even we have to draw a line
somewhere.

## Academic integrity

Analysing other people's excuses is the whole course; presenting someone
else's analysis as your own is not. Cite every source you build on — a real
excuse, a scholarly one, or an AI-generated one. Undeclared use of an AI tool
to write graded analysis is treated as a breach of academic integrity, the
same as any other undeclared authorship.

## Participation

You are expected to attend your weekly session and contribute — this is a
course about live argument, and it is hard to teach from the back row. If you
cannot attend, tell us before the session, not after; an absence excuse is,
appropriately, held to the same standard as everything else we study here.

## Getting help

Start with your weekly session. For anything that cannot wait until then, see
the [people page](/people/) for who to contact and how.
