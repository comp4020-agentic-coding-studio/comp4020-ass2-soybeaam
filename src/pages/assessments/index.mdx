Four pieces of assessed work, together worth 100% of your final mark.

They are one sequence rather than four separate tasks. The teardown takes a
real excuse apart using the taxonomy and grammar from the first four weeks.
The lab rebuilds one, turning a corporate non-apology into an apology that
actually works. The presentation puts you in front of the room defending a
reading of a case under live questioning. The capstone then asks for an excuse
of your own and a written defense against exactly the cross-examination the
presentation trained you to expect. Each assumes the one before it, so falling
behind on the first is expensive twice.

Dates below are the day each piece is due. Every deadline has a time of day as
well, shown in full on the brief itself — open the brief before you plan the
last night.
